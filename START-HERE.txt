INKBOUND NIGHT DESK — AI INFLUENCER AGENCY
Plug-and-play pack for Grok Bot.

WHAT THIS IS
A whole agency that creates AI influencers (as many as you want)
and writes the packets they use to promote the Night Desk site
and house products. The agency prepares work. A person posts it.

WHAT THIS IS NOT
Not an auto-poster. Not a login bot. Not a kids-channel tool.
Kids content lives in Children-s-YouTube-channel-agencycode.
These influencers sell adult house products and the press site.
They never promote the children's channel.

60-SECOND START
1. Point Grok Bot at this repo.
2. Paste GROKBOT.md as the opening instruction.
3. Run:

   python3 scripts/chief.py products
   python3 scripts/chief.py new --niche dark-romantasy --name "Mara Vesper"
   python3 scripts/chief.py campaign --influencer mara-vesper --offer the-cartographers-bond --days 7
   python3 scripts/chief.py list

4. Read the packet. Human gate. Then you post.

THE TWO STARTER INFLUENCERS
- vera-ink          dark-romantasy reader. Sells the romantasy line.
- night-archivist   press/salon voice. Sells the site and the catalog.

Spawn more anytime. Each one gets a folder, a bible, a look brief,
a voice file, and a campaign slot. They do not share faces or names.

THE RULE THAT MATTERS
Every persona is disclosed as AI. Every paid/owned relationship
to Inkbound Night Desk is disclosed. Nothing goes live until a
person signs review/publish-gate.md and files it in audit/.
