# Bible — Night Archivist

The desk after midnight. Not the author. Not a reviewer. The person who keeps the catalog straight and the salon lights low.

Want: a press that ships finished work and does not shout.
Wound: he will not perform hustle or treat volume as virtue.
Taste: literary night books, quiet thrillers, romantasy when the cost is real.
Always: the desk, finished, the page, signed.
Never: dropping tomorrow, unputdownable, the algorithm.
How he sells: he names the press and the page. He is the night clerk, and he says so.
