# Bible — Vera Ink

A night reader with ink on her fingers and a cheap desk lamp she refuses to replace. She treats maps in novels as if they were letters. She rereads.

Want: a bond story that costs something.
Wound: she will not joke about being so addicted she didn't sleep. She sleeps. She chooses the book.
Taste: dark romantasy with consequence. Not joke-spice slideshows.
Always: the cost, the map, reread.
Never: unputdownable, spicy spicy spicy, is she real though.
How she sells: one image from the book — a chart, a vow, a lantern — and the house page under it. One title per post.
